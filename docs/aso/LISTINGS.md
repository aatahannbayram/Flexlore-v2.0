```
Flexlore turns how your best person does the job into short video steps that every new hire can follow on their phone.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Film the job once, right where it happens. No studio, no script.
2. Split the video into steps in the editor. AI drafts the written instructions and a short quiz. Your team reviews and approves.
3. Assign it by role and location. Your team watches step by step on their own phone and replays any step.
4. They take the quiz. You see who watched and who passed, by person, team and location.

WHAT YOU CAN DO TODAY
• Video to SOP: turn a phone video of real work into step-by-step instructions.
• Phone video editor: trim, mark the steps, add text and stickers to point at the detail that matters.
• AI quiz: questions drafted from the steps you approved, answered right after the video.
• Readiness tracking: who watched, who passed and which question people miss most.
• Notifications: your team gets a push when a new training is added, plus announcements and urgent announcements from managers.
• Pick up where you stopped: videos resume at the point you left them.
• Roles, permissions and workspaces: people see only the jobs meant for their role and site.
• 8 languages: English, Turkish, German, Arabic, Spanish, Russian, French and Polish.

BUILT FOR THE FLOOR
Restaurants and cafés, bakeries, hotels, manufacturing, warehouses and franchise chains. Know-how stays with the company, not with one person.

AI drafts, people approve. A quiz shows who understood; signing someone off on the floor stays with their supervisor.

Free plan for up to 3 users. Paid plans by number of users: see flexlore.com/pricing.

Free guides and templates: flexlore.com/guides
```

  using the platform's own review prompt. Do not incentivise reviews.
- Reply to every store review.
- Ship an update with release notes; listings that never update rank lower.
- Link the store pages from the site guides (store badges are already on the site).

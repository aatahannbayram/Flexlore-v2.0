## 2. The problem

**Kicker:** Why it matters

### Know-how lives in someone's hands.
It leaves when they do.

Four short beats, one image each:

1. **Learned by shadowing.** New people copy whoever is on shift. Every handover bends the standard a little.
2. **Paid for in mistakes.** A step learned by guessing comes back as scrap, rework, returns, remakes and callbacks.
3. **Gone on their day off.** When the expert is on leave, setups drift and small errors repeat.
4. **Left at the customer's door.** Your installers run the orientation and drive away. The questions turn into support calls.

**Closing line:** The binder is on a shelf. The job is on the floor. Flexlore puts the job on every phone.

## 3. How it works (tour intro, supports the clickable app tour)

**Kicker:** Follow one job
### Film it. Approve it. Assign it.

1. **Record.** Your most experienced person films the job on a phone, the way it's really done. No studio. No script.
2. **Steps.** Flexlore cuts the video into steps and drafts the written instructions. You stay the editor.
3. **Quiz.** A short quiz is drafted from the steps. You see who understood, not just who pressed play.
4. **Readiness.** See who watched, who completed the work and who passed, by person, team, shift and location.

### Restaurants
**A new line cook has to plate your signature dish exactly like the head chef, during Friday rush.**
1. **Chef films it.** The head chef records the plating at the pass, from the cook's side of the counter.
2. **Steps and a quiz.** Flexlore cuts it into build order, sauce and garnish, then drafts a quiz on the allergen step.
3. **Cleared for the station.** The kitchen manager sees who passed before service starts.

### Manufacturing
**The changeover on press 2 depends on one senior operator, and that operator is on leave next week.**
1. **Filmed at the machine.** The lead operator records lock-out, tooling swap and the check on the opening part.
2. **Approved by engineering.** Steps and a safety quiz are reviewed before anyone is assigned.
3. **Shift planned on facts.** The supervisor sees who's cleared for which machine, by line and shift.

### Hotels
**Seasonal housekeepers arrive the week before opening and need to turn a room to your house standard.**
1. **One perfect room turn.** The head housekeeper films it, top to bottom, bathroom to bed.
2. **Short steps, quick check.** Each step gets a note; a short quiz confirms the standard landed.
3. **Ready before the guest.** The duty manager sees who's ready, by property and shift.

### Franchises
**A new menu item goes live Monday in every branch, and it has to look the same everywhere.**
1. **Filmed at the flagship.** The training team records the build once.
2. **Sent to every branch.** Same steps, same quiz, same day.
3. **Compared side by side.** Head office sees readiness branch by branch before launch.

### Warehouses
**Peak season brings temporary pickers who need to work safely within days, not weeks.**
1. **Filmed in the aisle.** The shift lead records receiving, picking and the equipment pre-use check.
2. **Safety confirmed.** A short quiz covers the handling steps before anyone drives a forklift.
3. **Zones staffed by readiness.** The lead sees who's ready for which zone and task.

### Bakeries & cafés
**Your early shift has nobody experienced on hand to show how the dough should feel.**
1. **Close up at the bench.** The head baker films proofing, shaping and scoring.
2. **One clip per step.** Each cue gets its own clip and note, plus a quiz on the ones that matter.
3. **Ready to open.** The owner sees who can open which shop, bake and bar.

### Field service & installation
**Your team installs a machine at a customer's site, runs the orientation, and drives away.**
1. **Filmed on the installed unit.** The installer records start-up, cleaning and daily checks on that exact machine.
2. **On the customer's phones.** The customer's operators get the steps and the quiz.
3. **Orientation that stays.** New shifts on the customer's side learn it the same way months later.

### Healthcare & clinics
**A new assistant has to prepare a treatment room exactly the way your clinic's protocol says.**
1. **Filmed by your senior staff.** Room prep and instrument handling, as your team really does it.
2. **Critical steps, checked.** Steps follow your protocol; a quiz confirms the ones that can't be skipped.
3. **Ready before working alone.** The clinic manager sees who has passed, by role and location.

### Retail
**A new associate starts the weekend of a promotion and needs the display, the till and returns down cold.**
1. **Filmed on the shop floor.** The store lead records the display set-up and the returns process.
2. **Same standard, every store.** Steps and a quiz go out to every location at once.
3. **Ready for the weekend.** The area manager sees readiness, store by store.

### Construction
**A new crew joins mid-project and has to run the pre-start check the way your site does it.**
1. **Filmed on site.** The foreman records the pre-start check and the task itself.
2. **Safety steps, confirmed.** A quiz covers the critical steps before work starts.
3. **Cleared by task.** The site manager sees who's cleared for which task, by crew.

### Auto service
**A junior technician is about to service a model they've never worked on.**
1. **Filmed in the bay.** The master technician records the job from the technician's side of the car.
2. **Every check in order.** Steps carry the checks and notes; a quiz confirms the order.
3. **Signed off by job.** The service manager sees who's ready for which job.

## 6. AI: what it does today, what's coming

**Kicker:** AI
### AI does the paperwork. Your experts keep the say.
Your team reviews and approves every draft before it goes out.

### Today
- **Video into steps.** Upload a phone video of the job. Flexlore cuts it into clear steps, so nobody scrubs through a long recording to find the bit they need.
- **Instructions, drafted.** Each step gets a written instruction drafted from the content. You edit, you approve.
- **Quizzes, written for you.** Flexlore drafts a short quiz from the steps. You see who understood the job, not just who watched it.
- **Multilingual content.** Run your content in the languages your team speaks, so the standard is the same whoever is on shift.

### Coming soon
Planned on our roadmap. No dates yet.

- **Your brand, your words.** *(FLE-122)* Your logo, colours and welcome screen in the app, with templates adapted to your roles, branches and terms. Your team sees your company, not ours.
- **Scan a QR, start the job.** *(FLE-123)* Every job gets a shareable link. Managers print a QR for a machine, a station or a branch door. Scan it and the right job opens. Orientation stays exactly where the work happens.
- **Ask your training data anything.** *(FLE-124)* An API, a Power BI connection and an MCP server for Claude and other AI assistants. Ask "Who isn't ready at branch 3?" and get the answer from your own Flexlore data, in your own tools.
